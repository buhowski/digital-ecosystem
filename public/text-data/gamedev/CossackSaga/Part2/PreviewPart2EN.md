#### Dark Fantasy / Action RPG

# Part II: `Ashes of the Beloved`

She is a martyr of church dungeons, snatched from the flames of execution at the cost of her lover's self-sacrifice. With a fragment of his soul in place of her heart, she turns her pain into a devastating force to shatter the chains of the Church's demonic cult. But to save her betrothed's life, she will have to sacrifice the world.
